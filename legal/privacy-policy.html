1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Doo Games Studio collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use Promptlyss, which is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play.

2. App Store & Google Play Requirements

Apple requires every app submission to include a privacy policy link in App Store Connect and within the app, and the policy must disclose exactly what data the App collects, why, and how it is retained or deleted. Google Play likewise mandates a public privacy policy URL in the Play Console plus an in-app link, backed by an accurate Data Safety form that explains how data is accessed, collected, used, and shared.

We keep this page published at the same URL that is submitted to both stores so their reviewers and your players always see the latest version.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

  • Optional account/profile details such as nickname, avatar choice, or player preferences.
  • Support requests and other communications you send to the developer.
  • Any photos, text, or other content you deliberately upload into the App.

3.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device identifiers, operating system version, device model, language, and locale.
  • Usage metrics such as session duration, screens viewed, in-app actions, and progression data.
  • Performance and diagnostics data, including crash logs and stability reports.
  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., IDFA or Advertising ID) if we serve ads; these are used for basic ad delivery and analytics and respect user opt-out settings.

3.3 Purchases and Subscriptions

  • Purchase metadata from Apple/Google, such as entitlement status or subscription tier; we do not receive your full payment card number.
  • Transaction history only as needed to confirm premium access, restore purchases, or handle support requests.

4. How We Use Information

  • Provide the App’s core functionality, remember your settings, and restore progress.
  • Process support inquiries, manage account issues, and respond to questions.
  • Monitor stability and improve reliability through analytics and crash reporting.
  • Deliver optional advertising in a way that respects your device-level tracking choices.
  • Comply with legal obligations and detect abuse or fraud (e.g., payment verification, security monitoring).

5. Sharing and Third Parties

  • Apple and Google receive purchase and subscription data required to validate store entitlements.
  • Third-party analytics and crash-reporting services (e.g., Firebase, GameAnalytics, Sentry) receive aggregated usage and stability information.
  • Advertising networks only receive the minimal identifiers necessary to show ads if ads are enabled.
  • We may disclose data when required by law, to prevent harm, or to enforce these Terms.

We do not sell personal information or use it for profiling outside of the purposes listed above.

6. Data Safety and Security Measures

We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, including encryption, secure backups, and limiting access to developer personnel. We also keep our Google Play Data Safety form and this policy synchronized; any change in how we collect or share data is reflected in both places.

7. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary to provide features, comply with legal obligations, and troubleshoot issues. Device and analytics data is retained for a limited period that balances product improvement with privacy.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without verified parental consent. If you believe we have unintentionally gathered such data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Send any requests to doogamesstudio@gmail.com; we will respond within a reasonable timeframe and always comply with applicable privacy laws.
